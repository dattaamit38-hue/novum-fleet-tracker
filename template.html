Live AIS setup

Live positions are collected for everyone around the clock from AISStream.io and loaded into this page automatically. You don't need to enter anything here. The free feed comes from coastal receivers, so ships far offshore keep their last AIS position or their projected position.

Optional: paste your own AISStream key to also try a direct connection from this browser. The key and numbers are saved only in this browser.
